# SpongeBob SquarePants

My favorite animated series is **SpongeBob SquarePants**, which follows the everyday adventures of an optimistic sea sponge living in the underwater city of Bikini Bottom.

## Why It's Great
* **Iconic Humor:** Packed with memorable quotes and slapstick comedy that hold up at any age.
* **Great Cast:** Characters like Patrick Star, Squidward Tentacles, and Mr. Krabs bring hilarious dynamics to every episode.
* **Nostalgia:** Classic episodes and songs remain timeless favorites.
